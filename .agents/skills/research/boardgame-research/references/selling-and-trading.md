# Selling and trading a board game collection (VN Facebook groups)

He clears parts of his collection in Vietnamese Facebook board game groups
(GDTT ở Phú Nhuận, HCM) and also takes trade offers from other sellers.

## Listing skeleton

```
<TITLE LINE — keywords buyers search: game names + "thanh lý" + khu vực>

1️⃣ <Game> — <price>đ
• Condition bullets: bọc hộp / full sleeve hãng / sleeve popcorn theo act /
  bung seal chưa chơi / like new
• One line of why it's attractive (setup nhanh, nhỏ gọn, coop 2 người...)

2️⃣ <next item>

🔄 <trade line>
📍 <location>
📩 <contact + "ảnh thật + timestamp bên dưới">
```

## Rules that keep him out of trouble

- Two audiences, two posts. A group that only allows authentic items gets the
  authentic list plus a "hàng chính hãng 100%" claim; other groups get the same
  list plus knockoffs, each carrying "hàng fake (replica)". Never merge them and
  never drop the label — unlabelled fakes get the post reported.
- In the mixed listing, scope the trade line so fakes are excluded
  ("món 3–4 bán thẳng, không trade").
- Keep his edits: when he pastes back a revised draft, reproduce it verbatim and
  append only the requested block. Do not re-add lines he deleted (shipping,
  extras) and do not "polish" his wording.
- Real-item groups require photos with a handwritten name + date; mention it so
  the post survives moderation. Also suggest close-ups of box corners/edges.
- Get product names exactly right — buyers search by name, and a wrong expansion
  name (e.g. Galactic Cruise "Advancements"/"Accommodations", Sail: Seafarers)
  silently kills reach. Search to confirm instead of guessing.
- Prices stay in his notation (3m2 = 3.200.000đ). If he wants speed, offer a
  "fix nhẹ" line for private chats rather than on a real-item group post.

## Evaluating a trade offer from another seller

1. List what's offered with the exact product (base vs deluxe vs 2nd edition,
   which expansion) — editions change value a lot.
2. For each title get: weight, playtime, official player count and the
   review-backed best count, plus the one-line feel.
3. Score each against his fixed table: always 3-4 players, solo dropped,
   euro mid-weight ~2.9-3.3 preferred but 4.x tolerated, likes conflict and
   coop, dislikes Arnak, already owns Dune Uprising.
4. Flag edition traps: a 1st edition of an older game where a modern rework
   exists (e.g. Kemet 1st ed vs Blood and Sand), a KS deluxe that is OOP and
   holds value, or a box whose expansion is sold separately.
5. End with a ranked verdict (take / consider / skip) and a concrete swap shape:
   which titles, how many, and whether cash should top up. Never leave the
   decision open.
6. Be honest that used VN prices have no fixed floor and that he should sanity
   check with a quick group search — do not invent market prices.
